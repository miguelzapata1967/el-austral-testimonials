About Us

Inspired by the strength and perspective of Aconcagua, we help our clients reach higher through data-driven decision-making.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower businesses of all sizes by transforming complex data into clear, actionable insights that drive smarter decisions, improve operational efficiency, reduce costs, and support sustainable growth.

We are committed to delivering accessible, reliable, and innovative data analytics, business intelligence, and technology solutions tailored to the unique needs of each client. Through automation, transparency, professional integrity, and continuous improvement, we strive to simplify business challenges, eliminate unnecessary complexity, and provide measurable value.

Our purpose is not simply to provide reports, dashboards, or technology, but to build lasting partnerships that help our clients better understand their businesses, identify opportunities, manage risks, and achieve their goals with confidence.

We believe every business deserves access to meaningful insights, regardless of its size, industry, or location.

Miguel A. Zapata, Founder — El Austral Consultant
